Data Science Project

Global Sanitation Data Analytics

DAT 250 – Data Science & Society

Overview

This project applied the data science lifecycle to World Bank and United Nations datasets to evaluate progress toward Sustainable Development Goal 6.2. Using Tableau dashboards, I analyzed sanitation, handwashing, and open-defecation trends across five countries and developed data-driven policy recommendations while considering ethical issues and AI applications for global infrastructure planning.


My Role

  • Applied the data science lifecycle to World Bank and UN datasets focused on SDG 6.2
  • Built Tableau dashboards analyzing sanitation, handwashing, and open-defecation trends
  • Compared progress across five countries to surface patterns and gaps
  • Developed data-driven policy recommendations for decision makers
  • Evaluated ethical considerations and AI applications for global infrastructure planning

Tools Used

Tableau Data Science Policy Visualization

Key Takeaways

This project strengthened my ability to move from raw global datasets to clear visual insights and actionable policy recommendations. It also reinforced the importance of ethical judgment when applying data science and AI to infrastructure and public health decisions that affect communities worldwide.

Data Science Project

Global Sanitation Data Analytics

DAT 250 – Data Science & Society

Overview

I used the data science lifecycle on World Bank and UN data to track Sustainable Development Goal 6.2. Tableau dashboards compared sanitation, handwashing, and open-defecation trends across five countries and supported policy recommendations.

Country views

Country-level Tableau views

Each country chart tracks three indicators over time so progress (or gaps) is easy to compare at a glance.

Cross-country

Cross-country comparison

Population-scale views from Our World in Data helped separate percentage gains from absolute need, especially where population growth outpaced infrastructure.

Role

My role

  • Applied the data science lifecycle to World Bank and UN SDG 6.2 datasets
  • Built Tableau dashboards for sanitation, handwashing, and open defecation
  • Compared five countries to surface patterns and policy-relevant gaps
  • Framed recommendations with ethical and AI considerations for infrastructure planning
Tools

Tools used

Tableau Data Science Policy Visualization
Takeaways

Key takeaways

Rate improvements and absolute population need can tell different stories. Clear visuals made those tradeoffs easier to explain to decision makers.

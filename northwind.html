Business Analytics Project

Northwind Traders Consulting Project

CIS 236 Honors AI in Business

Overview

Our team acted as business consultants for Northwind Traders, using analytics to identify opportunities for revenue growth, customer profitability, inventory optimization, and forecasting. We developed executive recommendations supported by Tableau dashboards and a vendor-ready Request for Proposal (RFP).


My Role

  • Project Manager for a four-person consulting team
  • Built Tableau dashboards
  • Created Excel pivot table analyses
  • Developed BLUF summaries
  • Co-authored the RFP
  • Used AI to validate recommendations and stress-test business conclusions

Tools Used

Tableau Excel ChatGPT Business Analysis Project Management

Key Takeaways

This project strengthened my skills in business analytics, executive communication, data visualization, consulting, and collaborative problem solving. It also reinforced the importance of combining analytical thinking with clear communication to support business decision-making.

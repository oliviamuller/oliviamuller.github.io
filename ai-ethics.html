Data Science Project

AI Ethics & Governance

DAT 250 – Data Science & Society

Overview

On a four-person DAT 250 panel, I researched AI bias and governance in recruiting. We examined how automated hiring tools can create unfair or opaque outcomes, then presented recommendations on mitigation, transparency, and workforce impact.

Focus

What we examined

  • Where bias enters automated screening and ranking systems
  • Why transparency and auditability matter for candidates and employers
  • Governance practices that keep high-stakes AI decisions accountable
Role

My role

  • Researched AI bias, governance, and ethics in recruiting
  • Helped shape evidence-based recommendations for the panel
  • Presented findings on responsible AI use and workforce implications
Tools

Tools used

AI Research Ethics Presentation
Takeaways

Key takeaways

Hiring AI needs clear governance and bias checks. Fair outcomes depend as much on process design as on model performance.

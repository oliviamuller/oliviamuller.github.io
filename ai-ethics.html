Data Science Project

AI Ethics & Governance

DAT 250 – Data Science & Society

Overview

As part of a four-person panel for DAT 250, I researched AI bias, governance, and ethics in recruiting. Our team examined how automated hiring tools can introduce unfair outcomes and presented evidence-based recommendations on bias mitigation, transparency, governance, and workforce implications.


My Role

  • Researched AI bias, governance, and ethics in recruiting as part of a four-person panel
  • Analyzed how automated hiring systems can produce unfair or opaque outcomes
  • Developed evidence-based recommendations on bias, transparency, and governance
  • Presented findings on responsible AI use and workforce implications

Tools Used

AI Research Ethics Presentation

Key Takeaways

This project deepened my understanding of responsible AI in high-stakes decisions like hiring. It reinforced the need for transparency, clear governance, and careful evaluation of bias so that AI systems support fair outcomes for people and organizations.
